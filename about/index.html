What is ConnectionsFrame?

ConnectionsFrame is a browser-based thinking tool to help you reason through the daily NYT Connections puzzle before committing to an answer.

The puzzle gives you 16 words and asks you to sort them into four groups of four, each sharing a hidden connection. ConnectionsFrame gives you a private workspace to explore and test those connections without pressure.

How it works

  1. Enter the 16 words from today's puzzle — type them in one by one, paste a comma- (or otherwise) delimited list into any box, or paste one word per line into the text area.
  2. Open the workspace. The left panel shows all 16 words as draggable tiles. The right panel has four empty group slots.
  3. Drag tiles from the left into a group on the right to test a potential connection. Drag them back, or between groups, as your thinking evolves.
  4. Name each group using the text field above it — write down your hypothesis for what the connection is.
  5. Predict the difficulty colour. NYT rates each group from easiest to hardest: Yellow Green Blue Purple. Pick which you think each group is.

Privacy

ConnectionsFrame stores everything in your browser's localStorage only — nothing is sent to any server. Multiple people can use the same deployed site at the same time without seeing or interfering with each other's thinking. Your session persists between page refreshes until you click Start Over.

Tips

  • Start with the groups you're most confident about — move those tiles first.
  • Use the group name field to capture your reasoning, not just the label.
  • If you're stuck, try colour-coding your guesses to see where your confidence is.
  • You can drag tiles between right-hand groups without going back to the left pool.
