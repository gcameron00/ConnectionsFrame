About
1 Using ConnectionsFrame
  1. Enter the 16 words from today's NYT Connections puzzle in Today's Words below.
  2. Click Move to Workbench once all 16 are filled — the button activates automatically.
  3. Left panel: all 16 words as draggable tiles. Right panel: four empty group slots.
  4. Drag tiles into groups to test a connection. Name each group and pick a predicted difficulty colour — Yellow (easiest) through Purple (hardest). Groups animate into colour order automatically.
  5. Double-click any tile in a group to return it to the word pool.
  6. You can return to Today's Words at any time to fix a typo — edits sync live to the workbench.
  7. Your progress saves automatically in this browser. Other users cannot see it.
