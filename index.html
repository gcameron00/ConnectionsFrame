How to use
  1. Enter the 16 words from today's NYT Connections puzzle using any method below.
  2. Click Start Thinking to open your workspace.
  3. Left panel: all 16 words as draggable tiles.
  4. Right panel: four groups — drag tiles in to build your hypotheses.
  5. Name each group and pick a predicted difficulty colour (Yellow = easiest → Purple = hardest).
  6. Drag tiles back to the left or between groups at any time.
  7. Your work is saved automatically in this browser only — other users can't see it.
